BIO

Data-Centric & Reliable Machine Learning

I am an Assistant Professor in Data Science and Engineering at Eötvös Loránd University. My research focuses on learning from imbalanced, complex, imperfect and limited datasets, with particular emphasis on synthetic data generation, advanced resampling, dataset characterization, anomaly detection and machine-learning evaluation.

My work has evolved from behavioral biometrics and synthetic data toward class imbalance and data-centric machine learning. More recently, I have contributed to research on anomaly-detection evaluation and culturally grounded LLM benchmarking, while exploring generative and agentic AI as a way to automate data-centric ML workflows.

I completed my PhD in 2025 at ELTE, and I am now building toward an independent research programme that connects data characterization, data quality, imbalanced learning and reliable evaluation. I am early in this trajectory and actively looking for collaborators, grant partners and visiting opportunities that can help accelerate it.

OPEN TO OPPORTUNITIES

Looking to connect and contribute.

I am an early-career researcher with an active and growing research agenda. Below are the kinds of opportunities I am currently open to. If any of these resonate with you, please reach out by email — I would be happy to explore what might be possible.

01 · GRANT APPLICATIONS

Collaborative proposals

I am interested in contributing to interdisciplinary consortia and grant proposals — European (Horizon Europe, Marie Curie) or national — where data-centric AI, reliable ML, synthetic data or anomaly detection is a core component. I can contribute as a researcher, work-package co-lead or partner institution representative.

02 · RESEARCH COLLABORATIONS

Joint research

Open to collaborative research on data quality, class imbalance, synthetic data generation, anomaly detection, ML evaluation and generative/agentic AI for data-centric workflows. I welcome both short-term joint publications and longer-term research partnerships.

03 · POSTDOC & VISITING

Research positions

Open to postdoctoral fellowships and visiting researcher positions at institutions working on data-centric AI, reliable ML or closely adjacent topics. I am particularly interested in environments with strong research culture and international visibility.

04 · INVITED TALKS & WORKSHOPS

Talks and seminars

Available to present on imbalanced learning, synthetic data and its evaluation, anomaly detection (ASOI), data-centric machine learning and generative AI for data workflows. Contact me by email to discuss venue, format and topic.
