Profile

I am an Assistant Professor in Data Science and Engineering at Eötvös Loránd University. My research focuses on learning from difficult data, with particular emphasis on imbalanced learning, synthetic data generation, anomaly detection and reliable machine-learning evaluation.

My current research direction is broader: Data-Centric and Reliable Machine Learning. I am interested in how generative and agentic AI can help automate the diagnosis and transformation of datasets and make machine-learning workflows more robust.
