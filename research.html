RESEARCH

Understanding difficult data.
Building more reliable AI.

My research is centered on a data-first view of machine learning: understand the data, characterize its difficulties, transform it when necessary, and evaluate whether the resulting learning system is reliable.

RESEARCH EVOLUTION

From early experiments to data-centric & reliable ML.

A research trajectory shaped by a recurring question: how can better understanding and transformation of data improve machine learning?

2019–2021
FOUNDATIONS

Behavioral biometrics · synthetic data · computer vision

Early work explored keystroke dynamics, data generation and supervised computer-vision problems, building a foundation in applied machine learning and data preparation.

2021–2024
DATA-LEVEL LEARNING

Class imbalance · resampling · GANs · dataset characterization

The research focus moved toward difficult class distributions, synthetic minority data, directed undersampling and advanced resampling strategies.

2024–2026
RELIABILITY

Advanced resampling · anomaly detection · intrinsic evaluation

Recent work extends the data-centric perspective beyond class imbalance toward anomaly separation, overlap and evaluation when reliable labels are unavailable.

NOW
CURRENT RESEARCH IDENTITY

Data-Centric & Reliable Machine Learning

The current programme connects data characterization, data quality, imbalance, synthetic data, anomaly detection and reliable evaluation into a coherent data-first research direction.

EMERGING
FUTURE DIRECTION

Generative AI · Agentic AI · automated data-centric ML

An emerging direction is to use generative and agentic systems to diagnose datasets, identify difficult regions, select data transformations, generate and validate synthetic data, and support end-to-end ML workflows.

01 · DATA-CENTRIC AI

Make the dataset part of the model design.

Data-centric machine learning asks what can be learned by understanding and improving the dataset itself. My work connects dataset structure, class distributions, overlap, preprocessing and representation with downstream learning behavior.

02 · SYNTHETIC DATA

Generate data that is useful, not merely plausible.

I have investigated synthetic data generation for biomedical and imbalanced classification problems, including GAN-based tabular generation and adaptive sample generation around minority regions.

03 · RELIABLE ML

Evaluate learning systems under imperfect assumptions.

Reliability in my research includes appropriate evaluation, robustness to difficult distributions, and understanding when conventional metrics or labels are insufficient.

04 · IMBALANCED LEARNING

Learn from rare and underrepresented patterns.

My doctoral research focused on advanced data-level solutions for class imbalance, including adaptive oversampling, directed undersampling, minority-region discovery and synthetic sample generation.

05 · ANOMALY DETECTION

Measure anomalies when labels are missing.

The ASOI work extends this data-centric perspective to unsupervised anomaly detection by introducing intrinsic measures of separation and distributional overlap that do not require ground-truth anomaly labels.

06 · EMERGING

Generative & agentic AI for data-centric workflows.

This is an emerging direction: using generative and agentic AI to automate dataset diagnosis, data-quality analysis, synthetic-data generation, model development and evaluation. It is a future research direction rather than a claim of an established project.
