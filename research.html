01

Data-Centric AI

I investigate machine learning from the perspective of the data: its distribution, quality, structure, imbalance, overlap and anomalies. The goal is to understand where learning fails and what can be changed at the data level.

02

Synthetic Data

My work explores synthetic data generation as a practical tool for data scarcity and difficult distributions, including GAN-based approaches and data augmentation strategies.

03

Reliable Machine Learning

Reliability connects my work on data preparation, model evaluation and difficult learning conditions. I am particularly interested in methods that remain meaningful when assumptions such as balanced classes or ground-truth labels break down.

04

Imbalanced Learning

My doctoral research focused on advanced resampling solutions for class imbalance, including oversampling, directed undersampling, synthetic generation and cluster-based approaches.

05

Anomaly Detection

Recent work extends the data-centric perspective to unsupervised anomaly detection, including intrinsic evaluation when labeled ground truth is unavailable.

06 · EMERGING

Generative & Agentic AI

I am exploring how generative and agentic AI could automate data-centric ML workflows: diagnosing datasets, identifying difficult regions, generating data, selecting transformations and evaluating the resulting pipeline.
