ASSISTANT PROFESSOR · ELTE · BUDAPEST

Making machine learning more reliable through better data.

I am an Assistant Professor in Data Science and Engineering at Eötvös Loránd University, Budapest. My research asks what happens when the data is difficult — imbalanced, complex, limited, or poorly understood — and how understanding data more deeply makes the resulting learning system more reliable.

I am an early-career researcher actively building my research network. If you are working on related problems, looking for a collaborator, or have an opportunity that might fit, I would be glad to hear from you.

RESEARCH APPROACH

The dataset is not a fixed given — it is the first variable in the learning problem.

Understanding the structure, quality and difficulty of data before training is not a preprocessing step — it is the core of the research. Characterize first. Transform where necessary. Evaluate honestly.
